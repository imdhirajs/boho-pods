06 From Idea to Installation

How We
Build Yours.

Five stages. Thirty days on-site. Revenue starts the day after handover.

1
Week 1 · Remote

Consultation

A 45-minute call to understand your land, vision, and investment parameters. We come prepared with site-specific precedents.

2
Week 2 · On-Site

Site Survey

Our structural engineer visits. Soil testing, orientation analysis, utilities assessment. Feasibility report within 7 days.

3
Weeks 3–6 · Factory

Pod Construction

Your pod is built in our controlled factory environment. Weekly photo updates. Zero on-site fabrication mess.

4
Weeks 7–8 · Your Land

Site Preparation

Foundation, utilities, access path. Our ground team coordinates with your local contractors to minimise disruption.

5
Day 30 · Handover

Keys In Hand

Final assembly, snagging, commissioning, full walk-through. Keys in hand. Revenue begins the next morning.
